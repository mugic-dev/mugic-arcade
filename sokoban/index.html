倉庫番 SŌKOBAN

Point the MUGIC straight ahead and click Calibrate center. Turn it left/right (yaw) or tip it up/down (pitch) to the “Step at” angle for one step, then come back toward center for the next. A flick undoes the last move (or goes to the next level once solved). Keyboard: arrows move, U undo, R restart, N / P next / previous level.
