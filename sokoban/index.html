倉庫番 SŌKOBAN

GLOBAL TOP 10

Click Connect, then Calibrate center. Each flick is one step: flick left/right (yaw) or up/down (pitch). Undo / Restart are the buttons under the board; any flick goes to the next level once solved. Hard mode: no undo, only restart — and only Hard scores are saved. Tune “Flick at” on the energy meter and “Min turn” (how much the flick must turn to count). Keyboard: arrows move, U undo, R restart, N / P next / previous level.
