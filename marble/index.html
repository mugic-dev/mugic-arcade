MARBLE RUSH

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC flat and click Calibrate center. Tilt it to roll the marble (roll: left/right on screen, tip forward: up the screen); ramps speed it up. Race down each course to the checkered finish before the clock runs out — leftover time carries over. Roll off an edge into the void, or drop too far, and the marble respawns at the last checkpoint (the clock keeps going). Green slinkies knock you around. Keyboard (not ranked): arrows.
