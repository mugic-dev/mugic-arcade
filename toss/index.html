PAPER TOSS

GLOBAL TOP 10 — LONGEST STREAK

Connect, point the MUGIC at the bin and click Calibrate center. Aim by pointing left/right (yaw: the dotted line on the floor follows), then make a throwing flick: the harder the throw, the further the paper flies. Watch the wind. One miss ends the streak, and after a few baskets the bin starts to move. If throw strength is hard to control, switch "Power from" to tilt: the paper then goes further the more you tip the MUGIC up as you throw. Keyboard (practice, not posted): ← → aim, hold space to charge, release to throw.
