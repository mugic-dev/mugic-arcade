PAPER FLING

GLOBAL TOP 10 — LONGEST STREAK

Connect, point the MUGIC at the bin and click Calibrate center. Set up the throw, then make a throwing flick: point left/right (yaw) to aim — the dotted line on the floor follows; tip up (pitch) for a higher, steeper arc (level = flat 45°, tipped up 25° = 70° lob: drops in more easily but the wind has longer to push it); twist (roll) to put spin on it, which curves the paper — use it against the wind. The harder the throw, the further the paper flies. One miss ends the streak, and after a few baskets the bin starts to move. If throw strength is hard to control, switch "Power from" to tilt: tipping up then sets the distance (and the arc stays at 60°). Keyboard (practice, not posted): ← → aim, hold space to charge, release to throw.
