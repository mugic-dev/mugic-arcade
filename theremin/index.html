Hold the MUGIC level, pointing ahead, and click Calibrate center: that pose plays the middle of the piece's range. Tilt up for higher notes, down for lower ones (about 3–5° per semitone, depending on the piece). Roll controls the volume: level is full, twist away (+) to fade to silence between phrases, like a thereminist's left hand. (Set "Roll controls" to vibrato to make roll the vibrato depth instead.) A small wobble of the hand is real vibrato. Keep the glowing dot on the bars: green is in tune, blue close, red off. The guide tone quietly plays the melody so you can play by ear. No MUGIC: move the mouse up and down over the stage, hold the button to sound. Notes were converted from public-domain scores; tell us if a note sounds wrong.
