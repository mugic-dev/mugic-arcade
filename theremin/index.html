THEREMIN HERO

GLOBAL TOP 10

Hold the MUGIC level, pointing ahead, and click Calibrate center: that pose plays the middle of the piece's range. Tilt up for higher notes, down for lower ones (about 3–5° per semitone, depending on the piece). Roll controls the volume: level is full, twist away (+) to fade to silence between phrases, like a thereminist's left hand. (Set "Roll controls" to vibrato to make roll the vibrato depth instead.) A small wobble of the hand is real vibrato (〰 by the dot when it's detected). Keep the glowing dot on the bars: green is in tune, blue close, red off. On Normal and Hard the score's dynamics show above the lane (p, f, hairpins), the bars grow with the loudness and the volume meter shows a green band: twist to match it. A wave on a bar asks for vibrato (espressivo passages; on Hard, every long note). Normal: in tune counts 80%, dynamics 10%, vibrato 10%. Hard: a moment counts only when all are right. With vibrato, the note you center on is what's graded. The guide tone quietly plays the melody so you can play by ear. No MUGIC: move the mouse up and down over the stage, hold the button to sound.
