RESCUE CHOPPER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. Roll flies left/right (the further, the faster), tip up to climb and down to descend. Hold roll level to hover: the chopper turns to face you and its shots drop as bombs (for tanks). Flick to fire. Shoot the barracks open, land gently near the hostages (not on them!) so they can run aboard — 16 at most — and fly them back to base on the right. Tanks shell you on the ground; jets join later. Keyboard (practice, not posted): arrows fly, space fire.
