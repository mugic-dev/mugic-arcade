SKY SHIELD

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, point the MUGIC at the middle of the screen and click Calibrate center. Point it like a laser pointer — yaw (or roll) moves the crosshair left/right, pitch up/down — and flick to launch an interceptor from the nearest base with ammo. Its blast destroys incoming missiles; protect the six cities. Leftover ammo and cities score a bonus after each wave. Keyboard (not ranked): arrows aim, space fires.
