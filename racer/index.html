GRAND PRIX

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. Roll steers like a wheel (the further, the harder), tip forward (pitch down) for gas, tip back to brake. In Manual, flick to shift between LO and HI: LO pulls away, HI is needed for top speed. Take curves too fast and you slide wide; hitting a car or a roadside sign is a crash. Qualify on the first lap, then race 4 laps against the clock; each lap adds time. Only Manual games played with the MUGIC are posted. Keyboard (practice): ← → steer, ↑ gas, ↓ brake, space shift, 1/2 start.
