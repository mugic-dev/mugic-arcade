MUGIC MUNCHER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. It steers like a joystick: roll right/left or pitch up/down, and the further you tilt, the faster you go (level = stop). A turn you ask for early is taken at the next opening; a small sideways tilt is enough to ask for it, and one asked just past an opening still takes it. Flick or Enter to start. Keyboard: arrows.
