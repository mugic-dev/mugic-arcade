ROAD HOPPER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, point the MUGIC straight ahead and click Calibrate center. Each flick is one hop: flick up/down (pitch) or left/right (yaw). Cross the road, ride the logs and turtles across the river (the water is deadly, and some turtles dive), and fill the five homes before the timer runs out. Keyboard: arrows hop, Enter start.
