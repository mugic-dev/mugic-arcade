CONDUCTOR

GLOBAL TOP 10 — CONDUCTOR

Wear the MUGIC on your conducting hand, point it at the middle of the orchestra (the violas) and click Calibrate center. Raise your hand and hold it still: the orchestra gets ready. Each beat is a down-and-up dip of the hand — the bottom of the dip is where the orchestra plays; bigger beats play louder. Give a preparatory beat, then the downbeat: the orchestra comes in on the downbeat at the tempo of those two beats (within ±40% of the marked tempo), and then follows you. To cue a section, point at it ON its entry beat — swing toward it, or, if you already face it, dip lower than usual (Easy counts you in, shows a gold arrow and turns your beam green on target); a cue a beat or two early or late confuses them. Miss a cue and that section comes in timid. At the end, hold the last chord (the fermata) as long as you like, then cut it off with a flick. If your dips don't register, lower "Smallest beat" or set "Beat from" to flick. Record practice plays the piece at its marked tempo and saves your motion to a file (for tuning the game). Keyboard (practice, not posted): space beats, ← → or 1–5 point at a section, ↑ ↓ bigger / smaller beats.
