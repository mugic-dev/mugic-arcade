CONDUCTOR

GLOBAL TOP 10 — CONDUCTOR

Wear the MUGIC on your conducting hand, point it at the middle of the orchestra (the violas) and click Calibrate center. Each beat is a small down-and-up dip of the hand: the bottom of the dip is where the orchestra plays. Bigger beats play louder. To start, give a preparatory beat, then the downbeat: the orchestra comes in on the downbeat at the tempo of those two beats, and then follows you. To cue a section, point at it while you beat in the bar before it comes in. Cue it in time and it plays out with confidence; miss it and it comes in timid and unsure. At the end, hold the last chord (the fermata) as long as you like, then cut it off with a flick. If your dips don't register, set "Beat from" to flick and beat with small flicks. Keyboard (practice, not posted): space beats, ← → or 1–5 point at a section, ↑ ↓ bigger / smaller beats.
