SKY RINGS

GLOBAL TOP 10

Hold the MUGIC like a model plane, level, and click Calibrate center. The plane copies it: roll banks (and banking turns), tip the nose up to climb and down to dive, yaw is the rudder. Flick for a 3 s boost. Fly through the 15 rings in order, then the finish gate — 100 per ring plus a time bonus. Don't hit the ground! The yellow arrow and the radar point to the next ring. Keyboard (not ranked): ← → bank, ↑ ↓ climb/dive, A D rudder, W S throttle (also fine with the MUGIC), space start.
