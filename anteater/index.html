ANTEATER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Steer the tongue's tip through the anthill: roll right/left, pitch up/down, the further you tilt the faster it goes. Steering back along the tongue pulls it in; a flick snaps it all the way back. Eat every larva before the night is over. Only the tip eats: an ant touching the tongue anywhere else bites. Eat worms from behind, never head-on. A queen (bottom tunnel) clears every insect for a while; the spider comes out at night. Keyboard: arrows, space = retract.
