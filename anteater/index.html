ANTEATER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Steer the tongue's tip through the anthill: roll right/left, pitch up/down, the further you tilt the faster it goes. Tilt a little toward a shaft or side tunnel while moving and the tip turns into it at the next opening. Steering back along the tongue pulls it in; a flick snaps it all the way back. Eat every larva before the night is over. Ants keep to their tunnel and walk in from the edge and back out, some reaching far across. Only the tip eats: an ant touching the tongue anywhere else bites. Eat worms from behind, never head-on. The two queens sit in the bottom tunnel: eating one clears every insect for a while (only two per anthill); the spider comes out at night. Keyboard (any time, for testing — that game isn't posted to the global board): arrows, hold space = retract (let go to stop), Enter = start. With the MUGIC, a flick starts the retract and another flick stops it.
