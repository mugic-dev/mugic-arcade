DRAGON TOWER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. Roll walks left/right (the further, the faster). Hold it tipped down to crouch, tip it up to jump. Flick to strike: standing = high punch, crouched = low kick, in the air = jump kick. Grabbed? Shake the MUGIC (several quick flicks) to throw them off. Duck high knives, jump low ones, small fighters only fall to a low kick. Beat the boss at the end of each floor. Keyboard (practice, not posted): ← → walk, ↓ crouch, ↑ jump, X strike.
