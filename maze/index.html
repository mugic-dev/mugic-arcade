MARBLE MAZE

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC flat and click Calibrate center. The MUGIC is the board: tilt it and the steel ball rolls downhill (roll tilts left/right, tip forward rolls it up the screen). Steer around the holes to the green hole to drop to the next level. A wrong hole costs a ball and restarts the level; 3 balls. Levels pay 500 × level plus a time bonus. Keyboard (not ranked): arrows tilt.
