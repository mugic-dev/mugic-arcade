MOON LANDER

GLOBAL TOP 10

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. Roll turns the lander (the further, the faster), tip it forward (pitch down) to fire the engine — the further, the more thrust. Touch down on a flat pad, upright and slow (the readouts turn green). Fuel is all you have: a crash costs fuel, and the game ends when it runs out. Keyboard: ← → turn, ↑ thrust, Enter start.
