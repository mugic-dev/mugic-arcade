SLALOM

GLOBAL TOP 10 — FASTEST

Connect, hold the MUGIC level and click Calibrate center. Roll to carve left/right — the further, the sharper (and the more it slows you). Tip forward to tuck for speed, back to brake; flick to jump (over rocks). Pass between each pair of flags: a missed gate adds 5 s, trees, rocks and the fence knock you down. Fastest time wins. Keyboard (not ranked): ← → carve, ↑ tuck, ↓ brake, space jump.
